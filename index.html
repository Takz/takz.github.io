The pipeline

From your file to their headset

Send the files you already have. No cleanup first.

  1. STAGE 01

    Ingest

    Source files exactly as they are. We deal with the mess.

    IN · all major 3D formats
  2. STAGE 02

    Optimise

    Automated decimation, UV work, atlasing and draw-call reduction, against the Vision Pro frame budget.

    OUT · USDZ and GLB, Vision-ready
  3. STAGE 03

    Author

    Material groups detected for you. Assign finishes from a browser, no 3D software open.

    OUT · material sets you own
  4. STAGE 04

    Deliver

    Hosted and pulled on-device — at 1:1 in their room, or entered as a full space.

    OUT · Vision Pro, AR & VR

A single product takes minutes. A full space takes days to weeks — those get automation and hands-on work both, because a building is not a chandelier.
