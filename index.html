The pipeline

From your file to their headset

Four stages. Send the files you already have — no cleanup, no export instructions, no “please decimate first”.

  1. STAGE 01

    Ingest

    You send source files exactly as they exist today. We deal with the mess.

    IN · all major 3D formats
  2. STAGE 02

    Optimise

    Automated hierarchy flattening, mesh decimation with silhouette preservation, UV work, texture atlasing and draw-call reduction — all against the Vision Pro frame budget.

    OUT · USDZ and GLB, Vision-ready
  3. STAGE 03

    Author

    Material groups are detected automatically. You assign finishes, upload PBR textures or generate glass — from a browser, with no 3D software open.

    OUT · material sets you own
  4. STAGE 04

    Deliver

    Hosted and pulled on-device. Viewed at 1:1 in your client's own room, or entered as a full space at different times of day.

    OUT · Vision Pro, AR & VR

A single product takes minutes. A full space takes days to weeks — those get automation and hands-on work both, because a building is not a chandelier.
